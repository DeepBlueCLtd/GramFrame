Spectrograph Player samples

Each table below names a WAV instead of an image (spec 168). GramFrame decodes the recording, analyses it in the browser and shows the spectrogram as a waterfall: press ▶ and the newest sound enters at the top while the picture slides down. The whole recording is drawn from the moment it loads, so you can scroll through it and annotate it before playing a note (spec 171); pause to use the annotation tools, and they scroll with the gram when you resume. While it plays you can click the gram to pause it, or drag it to move around — it pauses under your hand and resumes where you let go — and the two contrast sliders on the bar lift a faint tonal out of the background without changing a single reading. Keys on a focused player: Space play/pause, J/L seek ±5 s, Home restart, M mute. Recordings: Work With Sounds via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0 — see ATTRIBUTION.md. Each WAV has a .wav.js sidecar beside it so this page also works when opened from the file system.
